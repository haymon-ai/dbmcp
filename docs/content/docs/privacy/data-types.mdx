---
title: Data types
description: Every kind of personal or sensitive data the built-in recognizers detect
---

Forty-six built-in regex recognisers ship by default, grouped into seven categories. Each recogniser is identified by an entity type that drives the `replace` operator's placeholder and is tagged with a category for opt-in subsetting via `DBMCP_PII_CATEGORIES` / `pii.categories`. Several recognisers ship with a checksum or context validator (Luhn, mod-11, ABA, EIN prefix, JWT header, PEM type-match, IRS ITIN range, NPI Luhn, DEA check digit, ICAO 9303 check digit, ISO 7064, KVNR / LANR / RVNR checksums, keyword context) that promotes the match to maximum confidence and rejects look-alikes.

**personal**
- `DATE_OF_BIRTH` — dates of birth (weak date pattern, keyword-gated)
- `EMAIL_ADDRESS` — email addresses

**financial**
- `BANK_ACCOUNT_UK` — UK bank account numbers (8–10 digits, keyword-gated)
- `BANK_ACCOUNT_US` — US bank account numbers (8–17 digits, keyword-gated)
- `CREDIT_CARD` — credit card numbers (Luhn-validated)
- `CVV` — card verification values (3–4 digits, keyword-gated)
- `IBAN_CODE` — International Bank Account Numbers
- `ROUTING_NUMBER_US` — US ABA routing transit numbers (checksum + keyword-gated)
- `SORT_CODE_UK` — UK sort codes (keyword-gated)

**government**
- `COMMERCIAL_REGISTER_DE` — German commercial register numbers (Handelsregister, HRA / HRB)
- `DRIVER_LICENSE_US` — US driver licence numbers (per-state formats, keyword-gated)
- `DRIVING_LICENCE_DE` — German driving licence numbers (EU-harmonised 11-character Führerschein)
- `DRIVING_LICENCE_UK` — UK DVLA driving licence numbers (16 characters)
- `HEALTH_INSURANCE_DE` — German health insurance numbers (KVNR, checksum-validated)
- `ID_CARD_DE` — German identity card numbers (Personalausweis, check-digit validated)
- `ITIN` — US Individual Taxpayer Identification Numbers (IRS range-validated)
- `LICENSE_PLATE_DE` — German vehicle registration plates (KFZ-Kennzeichen)
- `LIFETIME_PHYSICIAN_NUMBER_DE` — German lifetime physician numbers (LANR, checksum-validated)
- `MBI_US` — US Medicare Beneficiary Identifiers
- `MEDICAL_LICENSE_US` — US DEA certificate numbers (check-digit validated)
- `MEDICAL_PRACTICE_ID_DE` — German medical practice IDs (BSNR, 9 digits)
- `NHS_NUMBER` — UK NHS patient identifiers (mod-11 checksum)
- `NINO_UK` — UK National Insurance Numbers (HMRC prefix blocklist)
- `NPI_US` — US National Provider Identifiers (Luhn-validated)
- `PASSPORT_DE` — German passport numbers (ICAO 9303 check digit)
- `PASSPORT_UK` — UK passport numbers (9 digits, keyword-gated)
- `PASSPORT_US` — US passport numbers (keyword-gated)
- `SIN_CA` — Canadian Social Insurance Numbers (Luhn-validated)
- `SOCIAL_SECURITY_DE` — German pension insurance numbers (RVNR, checksum-validated)
- `TAX_ID_DE` — German tax identification numbers (Steuer-ID, ISO 7064 Mod 11, 10)
- `TAX_ID_EIN` — US Employer Identification Numbers (IRS prefix-validated)
- `TAX_NUMBER_DE` — German tax numbers (Steuernummer, ELSTER and state formats)
- `US_SSN` — United States Social Security Numbers
- `VAT_NUMBER` — EU / UK / NI VAT identifiers (per-country length validation)
- `VEHICLE_REGISTRATION_UK` — UK vehicle registration plates (current, prefix, and suffix formats)

**contact**
- `PHONE_NUMBER` — international phone numbers
- `POSTCODE_DE` — German postal codes (PLZ, keyword-gated)
- `POSTCODE_UK` — UK postcodes (keyword-gated)

**network**
- `IP_ADDRESS` — IPv4 and IPv6 addresses
- `MAC_ADDRESS` — MAC addresses (colon or dash separated)
- `URL` — http / https URLs

**digital-identity**
- `API_KEY` — five named provider tokens (AWS access, GitHub PAT, Stripe live, Google API, OpenAI) plus keyword-gated AWS secret access keys
- `JWT_TOKEN` — JSON Web Tokens (header `alg` field validated; signature **not** verified)
- `PASSWORD_HASH` — modular-crypt / PHC password hashes (bcrypt, sha-crypt, argon2)
- `PRIVATE_KEY` — PEM-fenced private key blocks (BEGIN / END type must match)

**crypto**
- `CRYPTO` — Bitcoin and similar wallet addresses

**NER (optional)**

The optional [ML/NER pass](/privacy/machine-learning) adds five entity types that have no fixed pattern. Which ones a model emits depends on its label set.

- `PERSON` — person names (`personal`)
- `LOCATION` — place and city names (`contact`)
- `ORGANIZATION` — company and institution names (`personal`)
- `NATIONALITY_RELIGION_POLITICS` — nationalities, religious and political groups (`personal`)
- `FACILITY` — buildings, airports, and similar named facilities (`contact`)
